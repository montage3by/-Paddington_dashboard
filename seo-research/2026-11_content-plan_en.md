# Paddington Park Dubai — SEO Content Plan

**Period:** 11–24 November 2026 · **Articles:** 6 · **Market:** Dubai (Arjan, Al Barsha South and nearby communities) · **Language:** English (Arabic insights used as supporting research)

---

## 1. Strategy at a glance

Dubai parents search in three main ways:
- **By brand.** Many of the top nursery searches in Dubai are the names of well-known nurseries.
- **By broad terms**, such as *nursery Dubai*, *British nursery* and *nursery near me*.
- **By questions they need answered before they decide:** *What is KHDA's role? What are EYFS and FS1? What does nursery cost? Is there a camp for my toddler this winter?*

The plan works on two levels:
1. **The homepage and Google Business Profile** compete for the broad commercial searches: *nursery Dubai*, *British nursery*, *nursery near me*.
2. **Six blog articles** answer the most-searched parent questions and lead readers to a tour, the price list or the camp programme.

Topics were chosen using Google Trends data for Dubai over the last 12 months, the week of 3–9 November, and five years of seasonality. They were also checked against what Paddington Park offers and what competitors already publish.

**Why now**
- **Licensing is in the news.** After Dubai Police closed 33 unlicensed nurseries on 6 November, searches for nurseries rose about 11% week on week. A news-related query about the crackdown became a "Breakout" search in Google Trends.
- **Winter camp season is starting.** Last year, Dubai searches for winter camps started rising in mid-November and peaked between 30 November and 13 December.
- **January is the second-busiest month for nursery searches.** Only late August and early September are busier, so families planning a January start are looking now.

## 4. Keywords and search demand

**How to read the demand index.** Google Trends shows relative interest, not exact search counts. We put all terms on one scale where *nursery dubai* (the 12-month average for Dubai) = **100**. Exact monthly volumes can be added from Google Keyword Planner before publication.

| Article | Main keywords | Demand index (Dubai, 12 months) | Search intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage (not an article) | nursery dubai · british nursery · nursery in dubai · nursery near me | 100 · 27 · 26 · 19 | Commercial, local |
| 1. KHDA | khda rating · khda nursery · licensed nursery | 40 (mostly about school ratings) | Informational, trust |
| 2. Winter camp | winter camp · winter camp dubai · winter camp near me | Seasonal: peaked at 143 last December | Commercial, seasonal |
| 3. Choosing a British nursery | how to choose a british nursery · best nursery dubai · nursery al barsha | Draws on the *british nursery* (27) and *nursery in dubai* (26) cluster | Commercial research |
| 4. EYFS vs Montessori | eyfs · british curriculum · montessori · fs1 / fs2 | 19 · 48 (mostly schools) · 52 (broad) · 19 | Informational |

| 6. Baby nursery | baby nursery · baby nursery near me · infant care dubai | 1.5, growing (*baby nursery near me* +130% year on year) | Commercial |

Each article has its own keyword group, so the articles don't compete with each other or with the homepage. Every article links to the homepage.

**Arabic.** In Dubai, Arabic nursery searches are much smaller than English ones: *حضانة* scores 6.5 against 100 for *nursery dubai*, and the word also means "child custody". Arabic-speaking parents often search for nurseries by name or look specifically for English-language nurseries. We therefore recommend focusing the blog on English and strengthening the Arabic homepage, rather than translating every article.

## 5. Articles

### Article 1 — Wed 11 November

**KHDA and Nurseries: How to Check a Nursery's Licence and What "KHDA Rating" Means**

**Why this topic.** *KHDA rating* is one of the most-searched education terms in Dubai. KHDA doesn't publish a comparable quality rating for nurseries the way it does for schools, and few pages explain this to parents. The 6 November crackdown on unlicensed nurseries has made the question urgent: Dubai Police are urging parents to verify licences before enrolling. For Paddington Park, it's an opportunity to show transparency.

**Structure**
- H1 KHDA and Nurseries
  - H2 What KHDA does, and doesn't do, for nurseries
  - H2 Is there a KHDA rating for nurseries?
  - H2 How to check a nursery's licence in 4 steps
    - H3 Ask for the KHDA permit number
    - H3 Find it in the official KHDA directory
    - H3 Check DHA approval for the nurse or clinic
    - H3 Look for the licence at reception
  - H2 Why it matters: the November 2026 crackdown
  - H2 Red flags of unlicensed childcare
  - H2 Paddington Park: our licence and standards
  - H2 FAQ

**SEO Title:** KHDA Nursery Licence & Ratings Explained | Dubai Parent Guide
**Meta Description:** Is there a KHDA rating for nurseries? How to check a Dubai nursery's KHDA licence, what to ask for on a tour, and red flags of unlicensed childcare.
**CTA:** Book a tour. We'll show you our KHDA permit and introduce our DHA-certified nurse.

## 6. Expected value for Paddington Park

- **Coverage of real parent searches.** The articles target topics with measurable demand in Dubai (KHDA, winter camps, British nurseries, EYFS/FS1, fees) and one growing niche (infant care). Today the site has a single page and can't appear for any of them.
- **Seasonal timing.** The winter camp article and the guide for new families are published ahead of their peak search periods: early December and January.
- **Trust.** Answering licensing and cost questions openly sets Paddington Park apart while licensing is in the news.
- **Leads.** Each article leads to an existing action: a tour, the price list or the WhatsApp camp request.

SEO results build over weeks and months, and they depend on indexing, competition and the site's overall authority. We don't promise specific rankings or traffic. We will track impressions, clicks and enquiries from each article in Google Search Console and Analytics.
