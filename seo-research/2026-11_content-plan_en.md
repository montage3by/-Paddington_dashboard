# Paddington Park Dubai — SEO Content Plan

**Period:** 11–24 November 2026 · **Articles:** 6 · **Market:** Dubai (Arjan, Al Barsha South and nearby communities) · **Language:** English (Arabic insights used as supporting research)

---

## 1. Strategy at a glance

Parents in Dubai don't search for "a nursery" in general. They search for answers to specific worries: *Is this nursery licensed? What does it really cost? Can my 2-month-old start? How will my toddler cope after the move?*

Right now, these searches are answered mostly by large directories and property portals. Few individual nurseries publish clear, expert answers to them, and none we found does so for Arjan and Al Barsha South.

This plan builds Paddington Park's first blog content around six of those questions. Each article:

- targets its own group of search terms, so the articles don't compete with each other or with the homepage;
- answers a real parent concern, using what Paddington Park actually offers: the purpose-built campus and park, British EYFS from 45 days to FS2, 8 AM–7 PM hours, the DHA-certified nurse, Arabic and Islamic studies, and holiday camps;
- ends with a clear next step: a tour, the price list or the camp programme.

**Timing.** The plan reflects what Dubai parents are dealing with this month:
- On 6 November, Dubai Police closed 33 unlicensed nurseries, and parents are now actively checking licences.
- Winter break starts on 14 December.
- January starts are coming up for relocating families.

  - H2 FAQ

## 6. Expected value for Paddington Park

- **Visibility where parents decide.** The plan adds content for licensing, fees, infant care, local and seasonal searches. Today the site has a single page and can't appear for these questions.
- **Trust.** Answering parents' safety and cost questions openly sets Paddington Park apart at a moment when licensing is in the news.
- **Leads.** Each article leads to an existing action: a tour booking, the price list or the WhatsApp camp request.
- **A foundation to build on.** Six articles start a blog that can grow into Arabic versions and further topics, such as FS1/FS2 in a nursery and EYFS explained.

SEO results build over weeks and months, and they depend on indexing, competition and the site's overall authority. We don't promise specific rankings or traffic figures. We will track impressions, clicks and enquiries from these pages in Google Search Console and Analytics.
